GlobalShield AI
GlobalShield AI is an enterprise-grade operational and financial risk intelligence platform designed for multinational enterprises. The system leverages automated AI agents to prevent multi-million dollar business losses.

🧠 System Architecture
Module 1: Automated Regulatory & Tax Compliance Guard

Function: Monitored ingestion of global corporate tax websites and official regulation feeds (e.g., GDPR updates, local tax authority changes).

AI Logic: Parses real-time legal updates, cross-references them with corporate policy documents, and triggers instant alerts for compliance gaps to prevent financial penalties.

Module 2: Geopolitical & Climate Supply Chain Predictive Engine

Function: Ingests global live news API streams, weather patterns, and global shipping maritime route data.

AI Logic: Predicts potential route blockages, war-zone hazards, or extreme weather delays to automatically forecast supply chain exposure and recommend optimized alternative routes.

🚀 Cloud Deployment & Scalability
This system architecture is engineered to deploy seamlessly on cloud infrastructure, utilizing scalable LLM orchestration to serve enterprise corporate clients globally with high availability and robust security compliance.
